Preparedness Tips

Basic reminders to help households prepare ahead of time.

🎒

Prepare a go-bag

Keep drinking water, ready-to-eat food, a flashlight, batteries, basic first-aid supplies, and copies of important documents.

📻

Follow official updates

Listen to trusted local authorities and official weather or disaster agencies. Avoid sharing unverified reports.

👨‍👩‍👧

Make a family plan

Agree on a meeting point, emergency contacts, and how household members will communicate if separated.
