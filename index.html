🛡️ Emergency Preparedness and Safety Tips

🔥

Fire Safety

Keep emergency exits clear, avoid overloaded electrical outlets, and know your evacuation routes.

🌊

Flood Preparedness

Monitor weather updates, prepare emergency supplies, and move to higher ground when authorities advise evacuation.

🌎

Earthquake Safety

Remember Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Stay away from windows and follow official safety instructions.

🚑

Medical Emergency

Contact emergency responders when needed and provide accurate information about the location and situation.

🎒

Emergency Go Bag

Prepare drinking water, food, flashlight, batteries, first-aid supplies, medications, and important documents.
